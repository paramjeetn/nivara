## 4. Jury Q&A Defensibility Guide (`docs/QA_PREP.md`)

The comprehensive Q&A guide was authored and committed to `docs/QA_PREP.md`. It provides battle-tested 30-second answers and deep technical defenses for:

1. **"Isn't your system trained only on synthetic data?"**
   - *Defense:* Dual-validation methodology. Synthetic data provides known ground truth for precision, recall, and detection delay benchmarks; BDG2 external transfer validation proves 6.57% Weekday MAPE on real classroom meters.
2. **"Why did Seasonal-Naive achieve a better MAPE (8.27%) than Ridge (40.09%) and Prophet (40.47%)?"**
   - *Defense:* Rigid diurnal academic schedules mirror weekly cycles. Percentage error distortion on low-load weekends inflates MAPE for parametric models. Ridge and Prophet are indispensable because they simulate what-if scenarios and provide calibrated prediction intervals.
3. **"Why choose Ridge and Prophet instead of LSTM or Transformer?"**
   - *Defense:* Interpretability, sample efficiency on 28-day spans, zero GPU dependency, < 20ms latency, ASHRAE Guideline 14 statutory auditability, and out-of-the-box calibrated prediction intervals.
4. **"Can this system actually be deployed on a real campus tomorrow?"**
   - *Defense:* Yes. Demonstrated by the BYOD smart heuristic CSV mapper (regex alias mapping, automatic Watts->kW and Joules->kWh conversion, IST timezone normalization) and 5-rule DQE scorecard.
5. **"Why focus on closed-loop actionability over building 30 pages?"**
   - *Defense:* Facility managers suffer from dashboard fatigue. Depth beats breadth. The north star pipeline (`Problem -> Data -> AI -> Insight -> Action -> Verified Result`) solves the real operational problem.
6. **"How do you guarantee your AI won't turn off critical medical equipment?"**
   - *Defense:* Service-Impact Guard enforces non-curtailable protected loads (`life_safety`, `cold_chain`, `critical_it`) and ASHRAE 55 PMV thermal comfort boundaries, synthesizing safe alternatives.
7. **"What happens if conference Wi-Fi cuts out or Gemini hits a quota limit?"**
   - *Defense:* 100% air-gapped autonomy. Grounded offline AI fallback, 2D Plotly vector schematic fallback, page-level error boundaries, and sub-second caching.
8. **"How do you avoid hallucinating speculative savings numbers?"**
   - *Defense:* Standardized three-point monotonic savings ranges `{low, expected, high}`, 49 traceable assumptions, and IPMVP Option B continuous M&V with published hit-rate %.
9. **"How does your system model complex Indian electrical billing?"**
   - *Defense:* Indian Tariff Engine (`tariffs.py`) modeling OERC HT Commercial rules: 2-part tariffs, 80% contract demand floor, 3 ToD pricing bands, and statutory power factor penalties/rebates.
10. **"How does Nivara support different stakeholders?"**
    - *Defense:* Single-codebase role customization (Administrator, Operations, Sustainability) dynamically tailoring UI widgets and Gemini system prompts without code duplication.
