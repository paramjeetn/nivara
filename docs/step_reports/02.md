## 3. Verified Sampling Interval & Key Audit Findings

### 3.1 Verified Telemetry Sampling Interval
- **Audit Conclusion:** The true simulation and telemetry sampling interval is **5 minutes** (NOT 15 minutes).
- **Evidence:**
  1. `configs/simulation.yaml` defines `simulation.interval_minutes: 5`.
  2. `data/manifests/simulation_manifest.json` confirms `"interval_minutes": 5`.
  3. `data/generated/telemetry.csv` timestamp differences for every asset evaluate strictly to `0 days 00:05:00` (8,063 intervals for a 28-day period).
  4. Energy derivation math in `src/campus_energy/simulator/telemetry.py` explicitly calculates:
     `interval_energy_kwh = round(noisy_power / 12.0, 6)`
     where $12.0 = \frac{60 \text{ minutes}}{5 \text{ minutes}}$.
  5. Forward-looking 24-hour forecasts in `analytics/forecasting.py` and `analytics/forecasting_prophet.py` project $288$ steps ($288 \times 5 \text{ min} = 1440 \text{ min} = 24 \text{ hours}$).

### 3.2 Facility Asset Breakdown
- **University Campus (`data/generated` / `configs/simulation.yaml`):** 6 buildings (`BLD-A`, `BLD-B`, `BLD-ADM`, `BLD-LIB`, `BLD-HST`, `BLD-UTL`), 14 zones, 30 equipment items, 6 main meters, 6 building assets = **56 total assets**.
- **District Hospital (`data/hospital` / `configs/hospital.yaml`):** 4 buildings, 10 zones, 22 equipment items = **40 total assets**.
- **Government Office (`data/govt_office` / `configs/govt_office.yaml`):** 4 buildings, 8 zones, 20 equipment items = **36 total assets**.
- **Industrial Estate (`data/industrial` / `configs/industrial.yaml`):** 3 buildings, 6 zones, 18 equipment items = **30 total assets**.

### 3.3 Anomaly Event Catalog
The simulator dynamically injects **5 equipment/sensor events** and **2 water network events**:
- `lights_left_on`: Overnight classroom lighting active for 6 hours (Severity: `medium`).
- `hvac_left_on`: AC override running for 8 hours after hours (Severity: `medium`).
- `pump_no_flow`: Hydraulic water pump running dry with 0 LPM flow for 1.5 hours (Severity: `high`).
- `sensor_dropout`: Lighting meter drops offline with missing telemetry for 3 hours (Severity: `low`).
- `duplicate_reading`: MQTT network retry duplicating telemetry frames (Severity: `low`).
- `pipe_leak_overnight`: Concealed pipe leak (+35 LPM) between 01:00 and 05:00 across 4 nights.
- `burst_valve_leak`: Daytime sudden burst (+83.3 LPM) for 6 hours.

## 5. Known Limitations & Next Steps (Step 3)

### 5.1 Known Limitations
1. **Scattered Hard-Coded Literals:** Business constants are scattered across at least 8 Python files. Any update to tariffs or carbon factors requires tedious manual updates across multiple files, risking silent drift.
2. **Fixed Interval Assumptions:** Hard-coded divisors (`12.0`, `36`, `288`) mean changing `interval_minutes` in configuration would silently distort energy integration and rolling features.
3. **Inconsistent Fallbacks:**
   - Electricity tariff: `8.5` in dashboard vs `8.0` in `web/main.py`.
   - Power factor fallback: `0.85` in config vs `0.92` in `replay/live_feed.py`.
   - Ambient temp fallback: `32.0°C` in analytics vs `34.0°C` in explainability.

### 5.2 Next Steps: Step 3 (Assumptions Registry)
1. **Create `config/assumptions.yaml`:**
   Consolidate tariffs, emission factors, BEE benchmarks, comfort parameters, SLA target hours, and scoring weights into a centralized YAML registry.
2. **Implement Typed Loader in `src/campus_energy/config.py`:**
   Add Pydantic models (`TariffAssumptions`, `EmissionAssumptions`, `BeeBenchmarks`, `ComfortAssumptions`, `WorkOrderAssumptions`) and `load_assumptions()`.
3. **Refactor Codebase to Consume Centralized Assumptions:**
   Replace raw literals across `analytics/`, `dashboard/`, `simulator/`, and `web/` with imported registry parameters.
4. **Parametrize Sampling Calculations:**
   Replace `12.0`, `36`, and `288` with dynamic calculations derived from `config.simulation.interval_minutes`.
5. **Run Full Test Suite:**
   Verify that all 76 tests pass with the new centralized registry in place.
