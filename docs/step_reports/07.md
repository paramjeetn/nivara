## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

### 1.1 Objective
The central principle of the Knowledge Transfer is: **"Energy optimization must NEVER compromise human life, clinical safety, critical cold chains, research continuity, or occupant thermal comfort."**

In real-world campus and institutional energy management, naive optimization algorithms frequently cause critical failures. A typical cost-minimization algorithm seeking to shave peak demand or avoid high time-of-use tariffs will naturally identify large continuous electrical loads (such as hospital air-handling units, refrigeration compressors, or ventilation fans) and propose curtailing or shedding them. In a standard office building, a temporary setpoint rise causes mild discomfort; in a hospital **Intensive Care Unit (ICU)** or **Operating Theatre (OT)**, turning off an air handler disrupts positive pressure gradients and surgical sterility, endangering human lives. Similarly, cutting power to vaccine cold-chain refrigeration or industrial continuous process lines results in catastrophic inventory spoilage and equipment damage.

**Step 7 makes the core principle visible, deterministic, and software-enforced.** It introduces the **Service-Impact Guard** (`src/campus_energy/decision/`), a defense-in-depth safety engine that intercepts all automated recommendations, evaluates them against physical, clinical, and human-comfort constraints, and either certifies them as `🟢 Service-Safe` or intercepts them as `🛡️ Blocked by Service-Impact Guard` while providing actionable, constructive AI safe alternatives.

## 3. Data Models & Schemas (`src/campus_energy/decision/recommendation.py`)

### 3.1 `SavingsRange` & `SavingsBundle`
Recommendations present standardized uncertainty ranges for decision makers rather than single-point estimates:

```python
class SavingsRange(BaseModel):
    low: float = Field(description="Conservative lower-bound estimate")
    expected: float = Field(description="Nominal/expected best estimate")
    high: float = Field(description="Optimistic upper-bound estimate")

class SavingsBundle(BaseModel):
    kwh: SavingsRange = Field(description="Estimated electrical energy savings in kWh")
    inr: SavingsRange = Field(description="Estimated cost savings in Indian Rupees (INR)")
    co2_kg: SavingsRange = Field(description="Estimated greenhouse gas abatement in kg CO2e")
```

The model includes a convenient factory method `SavingsBundle.from_expected(kwh_expected, inr_per_kwh, co2_per_kwh, spread=0.20)` that automatically computes defensible tripartite savings using validated registry parameters.

### 3.2 `GuardResult`
Captures the evaluation outcome of the Service-Impact Guard:

```python
class GuardResult(BaseModel):
    status: Literal["pass", "blocked", "needs_review"]
    reason: str
    constraints_checked: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
    safe_alternative: Optional[str] = None
```

### 3.3 `Recommendation`
The canonical data contract for all automated decision-support outputs:

```python
class Recommendation(BaseModel):
    id: str
    facility_id: str
    type: str  # e.g. "anomaly_fix", "setpoint_adjustment", "schedule_shift", "peak_shaving"
    target: str  # asset_id or zone_id
    action_text: str  # plain language
    trigger: str  # anomaly id, forecast event, or scenario
    savings: SavingsBundle
    cost: float = 0.0
    payback_months: Optional[float] = None
    assumption_refs: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
    service_guard: Optional[GuardResult] = None
    owner_role: Literal["administrator", "operations", "sustainability"]
    lifecycle: Literal["proposed", "accepted", "work_order", "done", "verified"] = "proposed"
    confidence: float = 0.8
    is_estimate: bool = True  # mandatory decision-support disclaimer flag
```

## 4. Guard Architecture & Evaluation Pipeline

The Service-Impact Guard (`src/campus_energy/decision/guard.py`) inspects recommendations through four deterministic sequential constraint layers:

```
                            ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                            │           Proposed Recommendation            │
                            └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                                   │
                                                   ▼
                                 ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
                                 │ 1. Protected Loads Check          │
                                 │    (ICU, OT, Cold Chain, Process) │
                                 └─────────────────┬─────────────────┘
                                                   │
                          Curtails Protected Load? ├──► [BLOCKED] Safe Alternative: Non-curtailed operation;
                                                   │              upstream audit / turnaround maintenance
                                                   ▼
                                 ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
                                 │ 2. Thermal Comfort (ASHRAE 55)    │
                                 │    compute_pmv & Zone Bounds      │
                                 └─────────────────┬─────────────────┘
                                                   │
                              Predicted PMV > Max? ├──► [BLOCKED] Safe Alternative: Limit setpoint to +1.0°C
                                                   │              or pre-cool 20 min prior to occupancy
                                                   ▼
                                 ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
                                 │ 3. Scheduled & Real Occupancy     │
                                 │    Occupant Count & Space Usage   │
                                 └─────────────────┬─────────────────┘
                                                   │
                           Shuts Off Occupied Bay? ├──► [BLOCKED] Safe Alternative: Dim lighting to 70%
                                                   │              or apply night setback after 20:00
                                                   ▼
                                 ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
                                 │ 4. Life-Safety & Ventilation      │
                                 │    ACH, Pressurization & Exhaust  │
                                 └─────────────────┬─────────────────┘
                                                   │
                               Curtailed Critical? ├──► [BLOCKED] Safe Alternative: Maintain required ACH;
                                                   │              inspect HEPA filter ΔP
                                                   ▼
                                 ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
                                 │   🟢 All Constraints Satisfied    │
                                 │   Status: "pass" (Service-Safe)   │
                                 └───────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 4.1 Constraint 1: Protected Loads Check (`protected_load_check`)
- **Target Identification:** Resolves target asset ID or zone against `facility_profile.get_protected_load(...)`. Performs case-insensitive matching, semantic alias lookup, and keyword matching against clinical/industrial categories (`life_safety`, `cold_chain`, `critical_it`, `continuous_process`).
- **Curtailment Interception:** If an action attempts to curtail, shed, turn off, power down, cycle, or reschedule a non-curtailable protected load, it is immediately blocked.
- **Defensible Rationale:** Cites the specific facility name, protected load designation, asset category, and statutory/clinical mandate.

### 4.2 Constraint 2: Thermal Comfort & ASHRAE PMV Check (`pmv_comfort_check`)
- **Predictive Comfort Modeling:** When a recommendation proposes adjusting HVAC setpoints or indoor temperature, the guard resolves the specific zone's `ComfortBound` via `facility_profile.get_comfort_bound_for_zone(...)`.
- **Fanger PMV Model:** Invokes `compute_pmv(temp_c, humidity_pct, air_velocity_mps, metabolic_rate, clothing_clo)` to calculate the Predicted Mean Vote.
- **Enforcement:** If predicted PMV exceeds the zone's upper limit (e.g. $+0.5$ in university classrooms, $+0.2$ in hospital surgical suites) during occupied hours, the recommendation is blocked.

### 4.3 Constraint 3: Scheduled & Detected Occupancy Check (`occupancy_check`)
- **Occupant Protection:** Intercepts proposals to shut off primary lighting or HVAC in spaces that are currently occupied or scheduled for active human occupancy.
- **Off-Hours Clearance:** Legitimate after-hours shutdowns (e.g. classroom lighting shut off at 22:00 with zero occupancy) are verified and passed.

### 4.4 Constraint 4: Life-Safety & Critical Ventilation Check (`ventilation_check`)
- **Infection & Fume Control:** Blocks any reduction in ventilation fan speeds, air changes per hour (ACH), or room pressurization in operating theatres, isolation units, cleanrooms, and chemical extractors.

## 5. Safe Alternative Mechanics

A key differentiator of Nivara is that **the Service-Impact Guard never produces dead ends**. When a naive optimization proposal is blocked, the engine provides an actionable **Safe Alternative**:

| Constraint Trigger | Example Blocked Proposal | Generated Safe Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold Chain (`cold_chain`)** | Turn off vaccine refrigerator compressor for 2 hours during peak tariff window. | *"Maintain uninterrupted 24/7 power and cooling for vaccine/cold-chain refrigeration. Conduct thermal door seal inspection, optimize defrost cycle timing, or utilize thermal phase-change buffer storage during peak tariff windows."* |
| **Life Safety (`life_safety`)** | Curtail ICU life support & ventilator power by 35% during peak grid demand. | *"Maintain continuous uncurtailed operation for life-critical clinical load. Perform efficiency audit on upstream central chillers or schedule preventive filter maintenance during scheduled facility turnaround."* |
| **Thermal Comfort (`pmv_comfort_check`)** | Raise Classroom 101 AC setpoint to 28.5°C during active lecture hours. | *"Limit setpoint adjustment to +1.0°C (proposed 25.0°C keeps predicted PMV within [-0.50, +0.50] envelope) or pre-cool 20 minutes prior to occupancy."* |
| **Occupancy (`occupancy_check`)** | Shut off primary lighting in Classroom 101 during lecture hours to shave demand. | *"Dim lighting to 70% or apply night setback only after scheduled shift ends at 20:00."* |
| **Critical IT (`critical_it`)** | Curtail Campus Server Room precision cooling by 50% during peak grid window. | *"Maintain uncurtailed power to server and IT infrastructure. Optimize CRAC supply temperature by +0.5°C within ASHRAE TC9.9 guidelines or consolidate idle compute workloads."* |

- A single recommendation engine synthesizing proposals across **all 4 triggers**:
  1. Anomaly diagnostics (e.g. stuck dampers, lights left on, dry-running pumps)
  2. Tariff optimization & peak shaving (time-of-use demand shifting)
  3. Microgrid dispatch (Solar + BESS arbitrage)
  4. Thermal comfort tuning (ASHRAE 55 continuous setpoint optimization)
- Automatic batch-filtering through `apply_service_guard` before recommendations enter the Action Queue.
- Direct work-order lifecycle transitions (`proposed` $\to$ `accepted` $\to$ `work_order` $\to$ `done` $\to$ `verified`).
